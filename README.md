## 📊 Projects

### 1. Sales Performance Dashboard
**Tools:** Power BI, Excel

<img width="1657" height="961" alt="Sales Performance Dashboard" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d90941c8-d8b8-40df-bf3a-7789ee3c8aa4" />

A dashboard analyzing sales performance across products, regions, categories, and time to identify revenue drivers, underperformance, and growth opportunities.

**Business questions this dashboard answers:**
- What is overall sales performance?
- Which products and regions generate the most (and least) revenue?
- How do sales trend across the year?
- How are sales distributed across categories?
- What actions would increase revenue and improve consistency?

**Key metrics:**
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | $359.9M |
| Total Orders | 4.9K |
| Total Quantity Sold | 51.2K |
| Avg Order Value | $73.5K |
| Top Region | Western Cape – $77.7M |
| Top Product | Tea – $43.1M |
| Top Category | Food – 34% |

**Key insights:**
- Tea ($43.1M) and Desk ($42.7M) are the top-performing products; Printer and Tablet lag behind.
- Western Cape leads regional sales ($77.7M), followed by Gauteng ($71.1M); KwaZulu-Natal is lowest.
- Sales peak in November ($33.4M) and dip in July ($28.3M), showing clear seasonality.
- Revenue is well diversified across Food (34%), Office (33%), and Electronics (32%) — no single category dominates.
- The gap between top and bottom performers (by region/product) is relatively small, indicating a stable, balanced sales base.

**Recommendations:** Replicate Western Cape's success in other regions, plan inventory/marketing around the November peak, run mid-year promotions to counter the July dip, and use bundling/cross-selling to raise average order value.

### 2. HR Analytics Dashboard
**Tools:** Power BI, Excel

<img width="1655" height="960" alt="HR Analytics " src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/676a90aa-ed4d-430d-b2ab-775aa3294770" />

A dashboard analyzing employee attrition across departments, regions, job roles, overtime, satisfaction, and training to help HR prioritize retention efforts.

**Business questions this dashboard answers:**
- What is the overall attrition rate?
- Which departments, regions, and roles are most affected?
- Does overtime drive attrition?
- How do job satisfaction, performance, and salary relate to retention?
- What actions would most effectively reduce attrition?

**Key metrics:**
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Attrition Rate | 44.0% (220 of 500 employees) |
| Avg Salary | $53.9K |
| Avg Training Hours | 38.0 |
| Avg Job Satisfaction | 3.0 / 5 |
| Avg Performance Rating | 3.1 / 5 |
| Employees Working Overtime | 256 |

**Key insights:**
- Overall attrition (44.0%) is well above the healthy benchmark of 10–15%, signaling a major retention challenge.
- IT (51) and HR (45) have the highest departmental attrition; Sales and Operations (27 each) the lowest.
- Gauteng (48) and Eastern Cape (47) are the highest-attrition regions; Limpopo (40) the lowest.
- Employees working overtime show noticeably higher attrition, pointing to possible burnout.
- Assistants (51) and Analysts (48) have the highest attrition by role.
- Attrition is highest at satisfaction extremes (very low or high); Assistants with low satisfaction (levels 1–2) are the highest-risk group overall.
- Salary alone doesn't explain attrition — high attrition persists across multiple roles and regions regardless of pay.

**Recommendations:** Prioritize retention initiatives in IT and HR, investigate regional root causes in Gauteng/Eastern Cape, manage overtime and workload, and build targeted career development for Assistants and Analysts.

### 3. Financial Performance & Insights Dashboard
**Tools:** Power BI, Excel

<img width="1659" height="962" alt="Financial Dashboard" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/64527882-ecd7-4a9b-9cae-25343a9efd96" />

A dashboard translating raw financial data into an executive view of revenue, profit, expenses, budget discipline, and regional/departmental performance.

**Business questions this dashboard answers:**
- How is the business performing overall?
- How does revenue trend across the year?
- How does actual spend compare to budget?
- Which regions and departments drive (or drag) performance?
- What specific actions would improve profitability?

**Key metrics:**
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | $126.03M |
| Total Profit | $115.93M |
| Operating Expenses | $12.73M |
| Profit Margin | 91.98% |
| Budget Variance | $113.31M Favorable |

**Key insights:**
- Revenue and profit produced an exceptional 91.98% profit margin, reflecting strong cost management.
- Revenue peaks in March and October, with a noticeable dip in December.
- Actual revenue exceeded budget every month, producing a $113.31M total favorable variance.
- The North region contributes the most revenue ($53M); the South region the least ($17M).
- HR generates the highest departmental profit ($22M), followed by Marketing ($19M) and Sales ($17M); Operations trails at $13M.
- Operating expenses ($12.73M) are spread fairly evenly across HR, IT, Marketing, and Sales (roughly 14–15% each).

**Recommendations:** Replicate high-margin practices across the business, reinvest in what drives the March/October peaks, address the December dip with targeted offers, and continue investing in the North region while building growth strategies for the South.
